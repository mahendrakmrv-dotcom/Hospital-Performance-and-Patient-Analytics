# 🏥 Hospital Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Hospital Analytics Dashboard is an end-to-end healthcare analytics project designed to monitor hospital performance, operational efficiency, patient care quality, treatment costs, and revenue generation.

The dashboard provides interactive insights into patient visits, departmental performance, doctor efficiency, treatment expenses, readmission trends, and patient satisfaction metrics. The goal is to help healthcare administrators make data-driven decisions that improve operational effectiveness and patient outcomes.

---

## Business Problem

Healthcare organizations generate large volumes of operational and patient-related data. Without proper analysis, identifying inefficiencies, revenue opportunities, and quality-of-care issues becomes difficult.

This project addresses key questions such as:

* Which departments generate the highest revenue?
* Which departments experience the longest patient waiting times?
* How do treatment costs vary across treatment types?
* Which doctors receive the highest patient satisfaction ratings?
* What are the readmission trends across departments?
* How can hospital management improve patient care and operational efficiency?

## Dashboard Sections

---

### 2. Operational Efficiency Analysis

Tracks:

* Department-wise waiting times
* Doctor-wise waiting times
* Operational bottlenecks
* Service efficiency

Key Finding:

* Cardiology department recorded the highest average waiting time.
* Pediatrics maintained the lowest waiting time.

---

### 3. Treatment Cost Analysis

Analyzes:

* Department-wise treatment costs
* Treatment-type cost comparison

Key Finding:

* Surgical procedures have the highest average treatment cost.
* Observation treatments have the lowest average treatment cost.

---

### 4. Revenue Analysis

Tracks:

* Revenue by department
* Revenue by doctor
* Monthly revenue trends

Key Finding:

* Cardiology generated the highest revenue among all departments.

---

### 5. Patient Care Analysis

Monitors:

* Patient satisfaction ratings
* Readmission trends
* Department performance

Key Finding:

* Significant differences exist in satisfaction scores across doctors.
* Cardiology recorded the highest readmission volume.

## Key Insights

* Cardiology contributes the highest hospital revenue.
* Cardiology also experiences the highest waiting times and readmissions.
* Surgical treatments are significantly more expensive than other treatment types.
* Patient satisfaction varies considerably across doctors.
* Revenue remains concentrated within a few high-performing departments.
